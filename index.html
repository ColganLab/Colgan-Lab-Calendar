    📧 How to request a swap email
    1. Select your name using the "Who are you?" search bar at the top of the page.
    2. Open a presentation you would like to swap with directly from the calendar (not your own presentation).
    3. Inside the presentation window, click "Draft Swap Email".
    4. Your default email app will open with a pre-written swap request email addressed to the presenter.
    5. Review the message and send the email.
    Tip: If you do not see the "Draft Swap Email" button, make sure you selected your name first.
